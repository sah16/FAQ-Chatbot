What's the exit load on HDFC Small Cap Fund?

The exit load for the HDFC Small Cap Fund is currently set at 1.00% if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment.

No exit load is levied if the redemption or switch-out occurs after the completion of 1 year. This policy is designed to discourage short-term trading and protect long-term investors.
